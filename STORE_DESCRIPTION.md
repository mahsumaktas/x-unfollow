## English

**X Unfollow Tool** --- The smartest way to clean up your Twitter/X following list!

**Features:**
- **6 Modes** --- Non-Followers, Unfollow All, Inactive Accounts, Overactive, Bio Filter, Follow All
- **Whitelist** --- Protect important accounts from being unfollowed
- **Smart Speed Control** --- Slow, Normal & Fast modes with built-in delays to avoid rate limits
- **Unfollow Limit** --- Set a max number to stay safe
- **Bio Keyword Filter** --- Unfollow or keep accounts based on bio keywords (crypto, nft, bot, etc.)
- **Inactive Account Detection** --- Find and remove accounts that haven't tweeted in 30/60/90/180/365 days
- **Overactive Detection** --- Find accounts tweeting 30+ times per day
- **Follow All Mode** --- Bulk follow everyone on a following list
- **Min. Follower Filter** --- Skip accounts with enough followers
- **Rate Limit Detection** --- Automatically waits 30 minutes when rate limited, then resumes
- **Dark & Light Theme** --- Beautiful Liquid Glass UI that matches your preference
- **Multi-language** --- English, Türkçe, Español, Deutsch, Français, العربية, 日本語, Português, 中文
- **Activity Log** --- Track every action in real time
- **Unfollow History** --- See who you unfollowed and when
- **Export / Import** --- Backup and restore your settings & history
- **Modern UI** --- Glassmorphism design inspired by iOS 26

**How to Use:**
1. Go to your X.com following page (x.com/yourusername/following)
2. Click the extension icon
3. Choose your mode
4. Adjust speed, limit, and filters if needed
5. Click Start!

**Privacy First:**
- All data processed locally on your device
- No external servers or APIs
- No data collection or tracking
- Open source & transparent

**Safe Usage Tips:**
- Keep the tab in the foreground for best results
- X/Twitter has daily limits (~400 unfollows per day)
- Use the Slow speed mode for maximum safety
- Add important accounts to your whitelist before starting
- The extension will automatically pause for 30 minutes if rate limited

**FAQ:**
Q: What happens if I get rate limited?
A: The extension automatically detects rate limits after 3 consecutive failures and waits 30 minutes before resuming.

Q: Is my account safe?
A: Yes. The extension uses built-in delays and respects Twitter's rate limits. Use Slow mode for maximum safety.

Q: Does this extension collect my data?
A: No. All processing happens locally on your device. No data is sent to any server.

The developers are not responsible for any consequences of using this tool, including but not limited to account restrictions. Always follow X/Twitter's Terms of Service.
